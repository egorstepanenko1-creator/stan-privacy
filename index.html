Stan Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Stan Studio is a private tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Stan the Stickman to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • read statistics and analytics of the creator's own channel and videos;
  • read public comments left on the channel's videos and post replies to them as the channel;
  • update titles, descriptions and uploads of the channel's own videos when the creator asks.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to run the channel: to understand how videos perform and to reply to viewers.
  • Analytics snapshots, the comments the app replied to, and its replies are stored privately on the creator's own computer.
  • To write a reply, the text of a public comment and its thread may be sent to an AI language model provider. No other personal data is sent.
  • Data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles of viewers.

Your choices

Viewers can delete their own comments on YouTube at any time. The channel owner can revoke the app's access in the Google Account permissions page.

Stan Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the channel through its YouTube page.
